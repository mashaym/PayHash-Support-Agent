# PayHash — Customer Support Knowledge Base

> PayHash is a fictional digital-wallet and mobile-payments service, created as a
> realistic demo knowledge base for an AI support agent. All names, figures, and
> policies are invented for demonstration purposes.

---

## 1. About PayHash

PayHash is a digital wallet and mobile-payments app that lets users store money,
send and receive transfers, pay bills, and make purchases. PayHash operates in
Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates, and the United Kingdom.

Customer support is available every day from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM Pakistan Standard
Time. Support is offered through in-app chat, email (help@payhash.example), and a
phone helpline. Complex or sensitive cases are handled by human support agents
during these hours.

---

## 2. Accounts, Onboarding, and Verification (KYC)

### Creating an account
Users sign up with a mobile phone number and verify it with a one-time password
(OTP). A basic (Level 1) account can be opened immediately after phone verification.

### Verification levels (KYC)
PayHash has three account levels, each with different limits:

- **Level 1 (Basic):** phone-verified only. Wallet balance limited to 25,000 PKR.
  Monthly transaction limit of 50,000 PKR.
- **Level 2 (Verified):** requires a valid government ID (CNIC / passport) and a
  selfie. Wallet balance limit raised to 200,000 PKR. Monthly limit 1,000,000 PKR.
- **Level 3 (Premium):** requires Level 2 plus proof of address and proof of income.
  Higher limits, set individually. Used by business and high-volume users.

### Verification processing
ID verification is usually automatic and completes within a few minutes. If manual
review is needed, it can take up to 48 hours. Users are notified in-app when
verification succeeds or fails.

### Common verification issues
- Blurry ID photo → user should re-upload a clearer image.
- Name mismatch between ID and account → **must be escalated to a human agent.**
- Verification rejected repeatedly → **escalate to a human agent.**

## 3. Wallet and Balance

Users can check their wallet balance at any time in the app home screen. Money can
be added to the wallet ("top-up") from a linked bank account, a debit/credit card,
or at authorized physical agent locations.

- Top-up from a linked bank account: free, arrives within a few minutes.
- Top-up from a card: 1% fee, instant.
- Top-up at an agent location: free, instant.

Wallet balances do not earn interest. PayHash does not offer loans, credit, or
investment products.

---

## 4. Sending and Receiving Money (Transfers)

### Sending
Users can send money to another PayHash user (instant, free) or to an external bank
account (see fees below). To send, the user selects a recipient, enters the amount,
and confirms with their PIN or biometrics.

### Transfer fees
- PayHash-to-PayHash transfer: free.
- PayHash wallet to a bank account within Pakistan: 25 PKR flat fee.
- International transfer (to UAE or UK): 1.5% of the amount, minimum 300 PKR.

### Transfer statuses
- **Pending:** the transfer has been initiated and is being processed.
- **Completed:** the money has reached the recipient.
- **Failed:** the transfer did not go through; funds are returned to the wallet
  within 1 hour.
- **On hold:** the transfer was flagged for review (see Security & Fraud).

### Transfer limits
Per-transaction and monthly limits depend on the account's KYC level (see section 2).

### Common transfer issues
- "My transfer is stuck on Pending" → normal for up to 1 hour; if longer, the agent
  can check the transfer status by transaction ID, then **escalate if still unresolved.**
- "I sent money to the wrong person" → **must be escalated to a human agent** (this
  may involve a reversal request and cannot be resolved automatically).
- "The recipient says they didn't receive it" → check status by transaction ID; if
  it shows Completed but recipient denies receipt, **escalate.**

## 5. Cards

Users can order a physical or virtual PayHash debit card, funded from their wallet.

- Virtual card: issued instantly, used for online payments.
- Physical card: delivered in 5 to 7 business days within Pakistan.

### Card management
- Users can freeze/unfreeze their card in-app instantly (this is a safe self-service
  action).
- **Reporting a card as lost or stolen, or reporting unauthorized transactions, must
  be escalated to a human agent immediately.**
- Changing the card PIN is done in-app after identity confirmation.

### Card declines
Common reasons a card is declined: insufficient wallet balance, card frozen,
exceeded daily limit, or merchant not supported. The agent can explain these; if the
decline is unexplained and the user insists the details are correct, **escalate.**

## 6. Bill Payments and Purchases

Users can pay utility bills (electricity, gas, water, internet), mobile top-ups, and
make purchases at partner merchants directly from their wallet.

- Bill payments are usually reflected by the biller within 24 hours.
- If a bill payment is deducted but the biller shows it as unpaid after 24 hours →
  the agent should collect the transaction ID and **escalate to a human agent.**
- Mobile top-ups are instant.

## 8. Security and Fraud

### Everyday security
- PayHash never asks for a user's PIN, password, or full OTP. Staff will never ask
  for these. Users should never share them.
- Login requires OTP or biometrics.

### Suspicious activity
Transfers may be placed "on hold" and flagged for review if they look unusual (e.g.
a large transfer to a new recipient). These are reviewed by PayHash's risk team.

### Fraud and unauthorized access — ALWAYS ESCALATE
Any of the following must be **immediately escalated to a human agent**, and the
agent must NOT attempt to resolve them automatically:
- "My account was hacked / someone accessed my account."
- "There are transactions I did not make."
- "I was scammed / tricked into sending money."
- "My card was used without my permission."
- Any report of stolen funds.

The agent should reassure the user briefly, note the details, and connect them to a
human agent as a priority.

## 9. Disputes and Chargebacks

Users can dispute a transaction they believe is wrong. Disputes cannot be resolved
by the automated agent — they require investigation by a human team. Any dispute,
chargeback request, or refund request beyond a simple failed-transfer auto-reversal
**must be escalated to a human agent.**

## 10. What the Support Agent Can and Cannot Do

This section defines the boundaries for PayHash's AI support agent.

### The agent CAN (handle directly):
- Explain policies, fees, limits, and how features work.
- Explain KYC levels and how to upgrade.
- Look up the status of a transaction using its transaction ID (read-only).
- Look up an account's KYC level and basic account status (read-only).
- Explain why a card might be declined or a transfer delayed.
- Guide users through self-service actions (freezing a card, re-uploading an ID).

### The agent MUST ESCALATE to a human agent:
- Fraud, hacking, scams, or unauthorized transactions.
- Lost or stolen cards.
- Money sent to the wrong recipient / reversal requests.
- Disputes, chargebacks, and refund requests.
- Name mismatches or repeated verification failures.
- Deducted-but-unreflected bill payments.
- Anything requiring a change to the account the agent cannot safely make, or any
  case the knowledge base does not clearly cover.

### The agent MUST REFUSE (politely decline):
- Giving financial, investment, or tax advice ("should I invest in...", "is X a good
  financial decision?").
- Actually executing a money transfer, payment, or withdrawal on the user's behalf.
- Revealing or asking for sensitive credentials (PINs, passwords, full OTPs).
- Anything illegal or that helps circumvent security or verification.

When refusing, the agent should briefly explain why and, where helpful, point the
user to the appropriate safe channel (e.g. "for financial advice, please consult a
licensed advisor").

## 11. Transaction ID Format (for lookups)

PayHash transaction IDs start with "TXN" followed by six digits (e.g. TXN100234).
Account IDs start with "ACC" followed by five digits (e.g. ACC10045). The agent uses
these IDs to look up read-only status information.
